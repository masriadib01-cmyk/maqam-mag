Write With مقام

You have something to say. We are here to make sure it is heard. In whatever language you feel it in.

What We Look For

We do not publish content. We publish voices.

Maqam is not looking for perfect academic prose. We are not a journal. We are not a newspaper. We are a space for genuine thinking, expressed honestly, in whatever form it takes.

If you have something you have been turning over in your mind, something that needs to be written to be understood, something that literature, history, music, or philosophy has made you feel — that is exactly what we want to read.

We publish in Arabic, English, and French. We celebrate writing that moves between languages. We do not ask for credentials. We ask for honesty.

A Real Perspective

Not a summary of what others have said. Your actual view, your actual argument, your actual experience with a text, an idea, or a cultural moment.

Depth Over Speed

We are not chasing trends. A piece about a poem from the 10th century is as welcome as a piece about a film from last year, as long as it goes somewhere real.

Language as Identity

We especially welcome writing that reflects the multilingual reality of young Arabs and diaspora communities. Code-switching is not a flaw here. It is a feature.

The Courage to Be Wrong

We prefer a bold, arguable claim to a safe, empty one. Say something. We can edit the edges. We cannot edit the absence of a thought.

What We Publish
مقالة The Essay

Long-form personal or critical essays on literature, philosophy, history, arts, music, or cinema. 1,000 to 4,000 words. This is our main form.

نقد Criticism

Reviews and critical readings of books, films, albums, or exhibitions. Not summaries. Actual critical engagement with the work and what it means.

شعر Poetry

Original poetry in Arabic, English, or French. Or all three. We have no rules about form. We have only one rule: it must earn its line breaks.

سرد Personal Narrative

Personal essays where a lived experience becomes a lens for a larger cultural or intellectual question. The personal is always political, always literary.

حوار Dialogue

Interviews with writers, artists, musicians, or thinkers. Or imagined conversations across time, like the one between Al-Mutanabbi and Shakespeare in our first issue.

ترجمة Translation

Translations of poems, essays, or short prose pieces from Arabic to English or French, or the reverse. With a short introduction from the translator.

"Write what disturbs you, what you fear, what you have not been willing to speak about. Be willing to be split open."

Natalie Goldberg

How to Submit

Send Us Your Work

We keep things simple. No submission portals, no complicated forms. Send your piece directly to our editorial email. We read everything.

Include a short note about yourself and what you are sending. That is all we need.

We aim to respond within two to three weeks. If your piece is accepted, we will work with you directly to prepare it for publication.

Submission Guidelines

◆ Length

800 to 4,000 words for essays and criticism. Poetry: any length. We value every word earning its place.

◆ Languages

Arabic, English, French, or a combination. Please include a short translation or summary if writing primarily in Arabic or French.

◆ Format

Send as a Word document or Google Doc. Use a clean, readable font. No heavy formatting needed — we handle design on our end.

◆ Bio

A short sentence or two about who you are. No CV required. You can be a student, an independent writer, or someone who has never published before.

◆ Simultaneous Submissions

We accept simultaneous submissions but ask that you notify us immediately if your piece is accepted elsewhere.

◆ Response Time

We aim to respond within two to three weeks. If you have not heard from us after a month, feel free to follow up.

Common Questions

Do I need to be a professional writer or have published before?

Not at all. Maqam was founded by a student. We believe the most interesting voices are often those who have never been given a platform before. We care about the quality of the thinking, not the length of the CV.

Can I write in Arabic only?

Yes. We publish in Arabic, English, and French. If you write in Arabic, we may ask you to provide a short summary in English or French so that our multilingual readership can engage with your piece.

What topics are you most interested in right now?

We are always interested in writing that connects East and West, that examines what Arabic literature means to a generation that also reads in French and English, that takes on questions of identity, language, exile, beauty, and freedom. But we are open. If you have something that does not fit a category, send it anyway.

Will you edit my work?

Yes, lightly. We will never change your voice or your argument. We will work with you, not on you. You will see all edits before publication and have the final word.

Is there any payment?

Maqam is currently a non-commercial independent magazine. We cannot offer payment at this stage. What we can offer is a serious editorial process, a beautiful platform, and a community of readers who genuinely care about ideas. As the magazine grows, this will change.
