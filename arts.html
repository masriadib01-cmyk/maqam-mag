The Gallery

Three traditions. Three ways of seeing the world. Each one convinced that beauty was a form of truth.

◆ Arab Tradition Pattern, Repetition, and the Divine
◆ Persian Tradition Miniature, Color, and Paradise
◆ Calligraphy The Word as Image
◆ Western Tradition Light, Shadow, and the Human Figure
Three Traditions
Arab World الفن الإسلامي Islamic Art

Rooted in geometry, calligraphy, and the rejection of representation, Islamic art built an entirely original visual language — one that is abstract, mathematical, and deeply spiritual all at once.

Iran · Central Asia المنمنمات الفارسية Persian Miniature

Intimate, jewel-like, impossibly detailed — Persian miniatures are entire worlds compressed into a single page. They depict paradise, love, and war with the same serene precision.

Europe · France الفن الغربي Western Painting

From the Renaissance to Impressionism, Western painting developed an obsession with light, perspective, and the individual human body. It is an art of presence — of things as they appear to the eye.
