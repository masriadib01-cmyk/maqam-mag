Writer in Focus

Naguib Mahfouz and the Novel as a Map of the City

No writer understood the Arab city the way Mahfouz understood Cairo. His Cairo Trilogy is not just a family saga — it is an entire civilization compressed into three volumes, spanning colonialism, nationalism, and the slow fracturing of tradition under modernity. He gave Arabic literature its first Nobel Prize, and gave Cairo something more precious: immortality.

To read Mahfouz is to understand that the novel, in Arabic hands, is not an imported European form — it is a living instrument that the Arab world has made entirely its own.

"Home is not where you live but where they understand you."

Naguib Mahfouz
