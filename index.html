About Maqam

Our Station

مقام

In Arabic, maqam means station — a place of standing, of gravity, of earned presence. In music, it is a modal framework that holds infinite possibility within disciplined form. We chose this name deliberately.


Maqam is a bilingual magazine for those who believe that arts and humanities are not decorations — they are the architecture of how we understand ourselves. We publish long-form essays, criticism, and cultural inquiry in English and Arabic, from writers across the world.


We do not chase trends. We seek depth, beauty, and the friction of real ideas. If you have something worth saying, write with us.
