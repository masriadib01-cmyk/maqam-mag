Two Thinkers Across Time

470 – 399 BC · Athens Socrates سقراط

The father of Western philosophy never wrote a single word. Everything we know about him comes through others. He believed the unexamined life was not worth living, and he died for that belief.

"I know that I know nothing."

VS
1332 – 1406 · Tunisia Ibn Khaldun ابن خلدون

Born in Tunis, the founding father of sociology, historiography, and economics. His Muqaddimah remains one of the most original works of thought ever written. He understood civilization as a living organism, six centuries before anyone else did.

"The past resembles the future more than one drop of water resembles another."

"The unexamined life is not worth living. But what about the over-examined one?"
